Moduvela MicroAI

Experimental on-device business interpreter

Entirely offline, no API calls, no LLM and no GPU required. This demo uses a tiny hybrid of explicit business term rules and per-clause nearest-example matching, then proposes a Moduvela-compatible blueprint. It is not a full natural-language model.

Experimental: review and edit proposals before using them. No model inference or server calls. Do not enter personal or confidential business information.
